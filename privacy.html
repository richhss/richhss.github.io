Privacy policy

Applies to the private, single-user applications described on the home page. Last updated 2026-09-22.

Who uses these applications

Only Richard Schurman, on computers he controls. They are not published, distributed or made available to any other person.

What data they access

With Richard's explicit consent through Google's sign-in, the applications read and, where he asks, modify data in his own Google account: Gmail messages, Calendar events, Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks and Contacts. Access is limited to the scopes he approves on the Google consent screen.

How data is stored and shared

Google account data is processed on Richard's own computer and shown to him there. Sign-in tokens are stored on that computer. No account data is sold, shared with third parties, uploaded to a server operated by these applications, or used for advertising. Google API data is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the task at hand. Richard can revoke the applications' access at any time from his Google account permissions page, which invalidates the stored tokens; deleting the local token files has the same effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email listed on the application's Google consent screen.
